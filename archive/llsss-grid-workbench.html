A separate program by LuveelVoom, not part of rlife or LLSSS. It was written largely with an AI model (Claude) and is probably buggy, so check what it produces before relying on it. Questions and problems go to the partial cutter thread on the ConwayLife forums; say which tool you used and include its inputs and outputs.
